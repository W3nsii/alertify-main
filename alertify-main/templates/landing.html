How it works

Three steps to your safety plan

  1. 1

    Share your location

    Allow location access, pin yourself on the map, or simply pick your barangay. Nothing is collected without your permission.

  2. 2

    Tell us your situation

    Select the hazard and answer quick questions about water level, damage, injuries, and who is with you.

  3. 3

    Act on clear guidance

    Follow your instructions, call the right hotline, and head to the nearest evacuation center when advised.
